## Review focus
- Source dies before receipt; history persists without fake live sessions.
- Worktree/index/evidence changes between prepare, receipt and commit reject stale transfer.
- Two successors race or repeat acceptance; one wins, no double owner/version.
- Running/unknown workers prevent duplicate restarts during transfer.
- Read-only workers cannot be required to execute tests or approve their own implementation.
